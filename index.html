Quick Check

Fast — reaches Lv100 at 800,000 EXP.
Medium Fast — reaches Lv100 at 1,000,000 EXP (level³).
Medium Slow — slower early, catches up mid-game.
Slow — reaches Lv100 at 1,250,000 EXP.
Erratic — fast early, crawls mid-game, speeds up late.
Fluctuating — slow early, speeds up in the mid-to-late game.

Levels aren't capped — Erratic's growth rate freezes at its official Lv100 value past that point so totals keep climbing smoothly instead of dipping.
